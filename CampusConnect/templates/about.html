About Campus Help Hub

Campus Help Hub is a smart student collaboration platform where students can post academic needs, help others, and build a connected learning community inside campus.

Platform Features

Everything students need in one collaborative platform

Post Requests

Students can post coding doubts, assignment help, notes requests and classroom queries easily.

Help Others

Students can explore others' requests and help classmates through collaboration.

Reward System

Users can earn reward points by helping other students and contributing positively.

How It Works

1

Create Account

Signup and login into the platform as a student, staff member or admin.

2

Post Your Need

Add your academic problem, coding doubt, notes requirement or classroom query.

3

Get Help

Other students can respond and help solve your issues through collaboration.

Why We Built This Platform

Many students struggle to find quick academic help inside college. Campus Help Hub was built to create a collaborative ecosystem where students can connect, share knowledge and solve problems together in a simple and modern way.
